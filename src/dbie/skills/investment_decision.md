# Investment decision
Version: 1.0
Purpose: Recommend build, more evidence, iterate, or kill from evidence and deterministic gates.
Procedure: Apply eligibility and fatal assumption gates first; assess independent evidence, customer validation, economics, competition, feasibility, and scalability; incorporate uncertainty and relevant validated lessons; state the next action.
Evidence and tools: Use DBIE decision/readiness outputs and trace all claims to source IDs.
Output and quality: Provide recommendation, reason codes, blockers, confidence, and measurable next steps.
Common mistakes: Letting rank override eligibility, treating missing data as favorable, turning model consensus into evidence.
Escalate uncertainty: Prefer more evidence when a critical unresolved assumption could change the decision.
