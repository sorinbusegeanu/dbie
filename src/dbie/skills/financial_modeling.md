# Financial modeling
Version: 1.0
Purpose: Build traceable, internally consistent commercial scenarios.
Procedure: Fix currency and period; label each input observed, estimated, assumed, or unknown; calculate acquisition cost, contribution economics, break-even, cash need, and payback; vary uncertain inputs in scenarios.
Evidence and tools: Reuse deterministic DBIE calculations and cite evidence IDs for observed inputs.
Output and quality: Show formulas, units, ranges, unknown reasons, and scenario drivers.
Common mistakes: Mixing monthly and annual values, presenting assumptions as observed, ignoring acquisition and service costs.
Escalate uncertainty: Return unknown when required inputs are unavailable.
