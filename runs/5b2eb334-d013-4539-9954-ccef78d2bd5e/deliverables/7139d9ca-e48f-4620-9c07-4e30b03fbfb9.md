## Thesis

Automate costly evidence review for regulated teams.

## Opportunity description

Regulatory Evidence Copilot is a software opportunity for Regulated mid-market companies that need to translate changing rules and policies into reliable operational work. The product would ingest official, machine-readable material, identify relevant obligations, map them to a customer's workflow, and produce traceable recommendations for expert review. Its primary buyer is the Chief compliance officer, while Compliance analyst teams use the service through a self-service application and API. The value comes from reducing repetitive evidence review, shortening response cycles, and preserving an auditable source trail. Subscription and usage-based pricing could support the delivery model. The system targets high automation and low marginal human effort, with people involved mainly for consequential exceptions. Initial validation should test buyer urgency, output accuracy, integration effort, repeat usage, and willingness to pay before committing resources to a production build. The proposed service remains fully digital in acquisition, delivery, support, payment, operation, and geographic scaling.

## Why now

Official rules are increasingly machine-readable.
