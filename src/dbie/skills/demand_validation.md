# Demand validation
Version: 1.0
Purpose: Test whether a defined buyer has a costly problem and will act.
Procedure: Define buyer and falsifiable demand assumption; separate market evidence from product-specific validation; seek direct feedback and purchase behavior; record contrary evidence and coverage gaps.
Evidence and tools: Use source-backed DBIE research tools; preserve URLs, dates, excerpts, lineage, and evidence direction.
Output and quality: State what evidence supports each claim, its scope, independent sources, and what would falsify the assumption.
Common mistakes: Counting repeated reports as independent, treating clicks or stated interest as purchases.
Escalate uncertainty: Recommend an experiment when direct buyer evidence is missing or contradictory.
