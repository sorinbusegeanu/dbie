# Competitive analysis
Version: 1.0
Purpose: Identify alternatives and test whether the proposed product is meaningfully differentiated.
Procedure: Search direct competitors and substitutes; compare target buyer, job, price, workflow, and distribution; verify current claims from primary sources; distinguish absence of search results from absence of competitors.
Evidence and tools: Use web, company, and DBIE source tools; cite source identifiers and dates.
Output and quality: Provide a concise comparison and a testable differentiation claim.
Common mistakes: Calling a crowded market empty, relying on stale feature lists, confusing features with defensibility.
Escalate uncertainty: Mark competitor coverage incomplete when evidence is sparse.
