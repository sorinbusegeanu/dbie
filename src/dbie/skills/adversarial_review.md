# Adversarial business review
Version: 1.0
Purpose: Find evidence-backed reasons an opportunity could fail.
Procedure: Challenge buyer urgency, distribution, economics, feasibility, differentiation, compliance, and operating burden; distinguish verified contradictions from plausible risks; identify the cheapest decisive test.
Evidence and tools: Use source-backed DBIE tools and retrieve relevant historical lessons.
Output and quality: Give severity, rationale, evidence IDs, and a concrete mitigation or kill condition.
Common mistakes: Treating speculation as disproof, repeating the same objection, ignoring positive evidence.
Escalate uncertainty: Label unsupported attacks as hypotheses, not facts.
