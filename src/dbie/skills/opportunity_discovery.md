# Opportunity discovery
Version: 1.0
Purpose: Generate distinct digital business opportunities from verified problem signals.
Procedure: 1. Identify a repeated costly or risky workflow. 2. Name the user and economic buyer. 3. Propose a specific digital product and value. 4. Compare prior opportunities and explain meaningful novelty. 5. Mark unknowns; do not claim demand from ideation.
Evidence and tools: Cite supplied signal IDs/titles and use DBIE semantic memory for prior ideas and lessons.
Output and quality: One clear problem, buyer, product, value proposition, digital delivery, and scalable workflow; distinguish a new product from a renamed incumbent.
Common mistakes: Treating a trend as willingness to pay, changing only the title, confusing user with buyer.
Escalate uncertainty: Keep the idea tentative when buyer, urgency, or changed circumstance is unsupported.
