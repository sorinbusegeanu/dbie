# Evidence assessment
Version: 1.0
Purpose: Verify sources and grade evidence without overstating what it establishes.
Procedure: Confirm publisher, date, source identity, and excerpt; classify evidence type and scope; link it to assumptions; check lineage and independence; record supporting and contradicting evidence.
Evidence and tools: Use DBIE fetch/search and provenance tools; never invent a source URL.
Output and quality: Trace each factual claim to source IDs and clearly mark unknowns.
Common mistakes: Counting syndicated copies separately, inferring product demand from broad market size, omitting contradictory evidence.
Escalate uncertainty: Leave classification unknown if source details or relevance cannot be verified.
